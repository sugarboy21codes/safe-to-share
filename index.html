Safe-to-Share

Paste text or a CSV. Names, contact details, IDs and credentials become consistent placeholders, and you can swap the real values back in after the AI replies.

  • [PERSON_A] personal data
  • [API_KEY_1] credential
  • Kept value you marked safe

Runs entirely inside this page. No uploads, no API calls, no tracking. Detection uses pattern rules in your browser, so nothing leaves your device.
